SIMULATED POLICY — Cartside portfolio demonstration.

Unused items may be considered for return within 30 days of delivery. Sale items are exchange-only. Damaged, spoiled, missing, or unusual grocery-item cases require human review. Under this policy, an approved refund would return to the original payment method in 5–7 business days after inspection.

Cartside cannot determine actual eligibility, process refunds or payments, change orders, or track deliveries. The historical data contains no delivery dates, payment details, or refund records. For a real order, use the support channel where you placed it. Keep payment-card details and personal information out of the chat.
